# Training plan

## Routing

Any total of minutes or fees, a schedule, or a clash check -> calculate_training_plan. Never add numbers yourself.

## Limits

- If a facility name matches nothing or several facilities, the tool reports the problem instead of a total; ask which one.

## External use

Call `calculate_training_plan` for any total of minutes or fees, an end time or a clash check. Never add the numbers yourself.
