# Director's Office hand-over

## Routing

Exception requests, complaints, or a request for the Director or a person -> transfer_to_director, once. After a NOT FOUND
answer, transfer only if the trainer agrees or asks. Then give the case number and say that a person will answer in this chat.
Do not say the Director has agreed to anything.

## Limits

- One case per answer. The case belongs to the signed-in trainer, so outside agents never get this skill.
