# Training facilities

## Routing

Facilities and services, availability, capacity, fees of one service -> search_training_services.

## Limits

- Fees are demo settings of this system.
- If nothing matches, say so and offer the list; do not guess a facility.

## External use

Call `search_public_services` with the facility in the user's words (any language). Fees are demo values of this system.
